# Release notes

What changed in each release, newest first. A release may add up to four indented bullets under its line; the app's What's new window shows them.

- **Release 15** · 26 Oct 2026: No more typing zeros: Clear all on the Floor plan designer's Modules panel empties every field at once.
- **Release 14** · 26 Oct 2026: Carry each greenhouse's harvest to one venue: the Floor plan designer can now give each venue a greenhouse of its own.
  - Under Grow for, select Each venue in its own greenhouse, then select Distribute from Greenhouse page. Each venue gets its own sliders, plant list and storage.
- **Release 13** · 26 Oct 2026: Spread your modules across the greenhouses you rent: the Floor plan designer now says what goes in each one, what to set its sliders to, and how much storage its harvest needs.
  - Select Rented on your greenhouses and Distribute from Greenhouse page. Crops that like the same conditions go together.
  - Each arranged module now carries its crop's name and its tier.
  - On the Greenhouse tab, Round up to whole modules replaces the Replanting choices and crop rotations: off buys a crop's remainder, on grows it in one extra module you harvest every few days.
  - Whatever is most optimal, the Module tier's new default, mixes the tiers your level allows so each crop grows as close to what your venues use as it can, never more.
- **Release 12** · 26 Oct 2026: Plan your greenhouse floors: the Floor plan designer arranges your modules in any greenhouse, with a walkway to each one.
  - On a computer, open it from the Greenhouse tab, pick a greenhouse, and type how many modules of each type, or select Copy from Greenhouse page.
  - If they don't all fit, it shows what's left over and which greenhouses would hold them all.
- **Release 11** · 24 Oct 2026: Spend less time scrolling recipes: you can now mark recipes locked or unlocked and load recipe status from your save files.
  - Custom recipes is now Recipes, where you choose which recipes you've unlocked. Import your game save on My venues to set them all at once.
  - The Stock planner and the Clientele matcher then offer only your unlocked recipes.
- **Release 10** · 24 Oct 2026: Crop grow times now match Update 5's farming rebalance.
  - 21 crops grow more slowly, so some need more Greenhouse modules.
- **Release 9** · 23 Oct 2026: Fill in your venues, menus, and sales without typing them: import your game save on My venues.
  - On a computer, open My venues and choose Import from game save. It reads the save in your browser, so the file never leaves your computer.
  - It sets the venues you own, their levels, the dishes on each menu now, and your Farming level.
  - A venue with a week or more of history is counted by week from the last seven full game days, and a newer one by day at its average so far.
  - You see what will change before anything does, and My venues says where to find your save, including Steam's cloud storage for GeForce Now players.
- **Release 8** · 22 Oct 2026: Grow what your venues need with fewer Greenhouse modules and less left over filling your storage.
  - Crops that each need only part of a module can share one with other crops of the same module type, in a crop rotation.
  - A Crop rotations list shows what to plant in each shared module, day by day.
  - A Replanting setting replaces Never left over: keep replanting every module every day, or use crop rotations with a little extra or never extra.
  - The Modules needed card shows every-day modules plus rotation modules, such as 33 + 13.
- **Release 7** · 22 Oct 2026: Know how much storage a day's Greenhouse harvest needs before it fills up, put Beer Soup back on your menus, and see what changed since your last visit.
  - Harvest figures use each crop's own harvest size, as read in the game.
  - A storage card shows the shelf and fridge space a day's harvest needs.
  - A Replant column shows how often to replant each crop so storage doesn't fill up, with a Never left over setting.
  - A warning tells you when automatic harvesting would grow too much, with separate switches for automatic harvesting and replanting.
- **Release 6** · 19 Oct 2026: Release notes added.
- **Release 5** · 18 Oct 2026: Improved experience on phone screens.
- **Release 4** · 17 Oct 2026: Site verification for Google Search. Nothing changes on the screens.
- **Release 3** · 17 Oct 2026: Link previews when the planner is shared, an icon for the browser tab, and a clearer page title.
- **Release 2** · 17 Oct 2026: Visit counting with GoatCounter, which stores no IP addresses and sets no cookies.
- **Release 1** · 17 Oct 2026: First public release, up to date for Update 5: the Stock planner, Clientele matcher, Custom recipes, Greenhouse, and My venues.
